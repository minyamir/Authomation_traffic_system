5. How the simulated traffic lights should behave
Your crossing_controller.py and signal_optimizer.py should have separate responsibilities:

signal_optimizer.py: calculates a recommended vehicle green-light duration from traffic density.

crossing_controller.py: manages the actual simulated signal states and pedestrian crossing sequence.

Example sequence:

1. Vehicle GREEN — pedestrian RED

Vehicles may proceed; pedestrians wait.

2. Vehicle YELLOW — pedestrian RED

Prepare to stop vehicles.

3. All-red clearance

All conflicting movements stop for a configured interval.

4. Pedestrian WALK

Allow crossing only after the clearance interval and safety checks.

5. Pedestrian RED — then vehicle GREEN

Finish the crossing phase and restore vehicle movement after the required clearance.

This is a simulation for your hackathon, not a certified real-world traffic controller. Pedestrian detection alone cannot guarantee that the crossing is safe.
